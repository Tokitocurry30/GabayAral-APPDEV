Try changing your search or status filter, or add a new subject.

A Subject is a reusable catalog entry. A Subject Offering is a separate record that connects a Subject to a particular Section. One Subject may be used by multiple Offerings.
Archiving a Subject must preserve its historical academic records. The Backend will validate whether the Subject can be archived when related Offerings exist.

Sample subject records are used for Frontend testing. Changes are temporary and are not saved to PostgreSQL.
