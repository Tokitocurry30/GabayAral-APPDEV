School Information Review

Confirm the school workspace where the initial academic structure will be prepared.

The setup must remain within the active School Administrator's authorized school workspace.

To change the school information, use School Profile and Settings.

Grade-Level Spaces

Prepare the grade-level spaces that will organize your school's academic structure.

Each Grade-Level Space belongs to the active School. You can manage these records individually later.

Enter one Grade-Level Space per line. These are sample entry instructions, not final database validation rules.

Section Preparation

Organize sections under their corresponding Grade-Level Spaces.

Every Section must belong to its correct Grade-Level Space. Avoid creating a Section without its academic parent.

Enter one section per line. Identify its corresponding Grade-Level Space.

School Subject Library

Prepare the reusable subject definitions that your school may use in its offerings.

The Subject Library contains reusable subject definitions. It is different from Subject Offerings assigned to individual Sections.

Enter one subject name per line. Subject-specific fields and classifications will be handled in the Subject Library page.

Subject Offerings

Prepare the section-specific subject offerings that connect your academic structure.

Each Subject Offering is a separate record for a specific Section. The same subject in different Sections must not be treated as one shared Offering.

Use this demonstration format: Grade-Level Space | Section | Subject Teacher assignments are managed separately after offerings are prepared.
