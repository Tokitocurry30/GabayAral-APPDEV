Verification Documents

Verification Guidelines

Before submitting a request

  • Use the Parent Section Code issued by the school.
  • Provide the required identity and relationship evidence.
  • Ensure documents are clear and readable.
  • Wait for authorized staff verification.
  • A pending request does not grant child access.
