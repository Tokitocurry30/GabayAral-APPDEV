What does acknowledgment mean?

A clear distinction for Parent/Guardian records

Acknowledgment confirms that you have received or become aware of the specified announcement version. It does not mean that you agree with every statement or policy in the announcement.

If the school publishes a revised version that requires acknowledgment, the revised version may require a new confirmation.

These records are local demonstrations. Official acknowledgment history and timestamps must be stored and enforced by the Backend.
