ACCESSIBILITY AND PERSONALIZATION

EasyAccess

Adjust your display, reading and language preferences to make GabayAral easier to use.

Personal Settings
Your Accessibility Preferences These settings are personal to your Teacher account. You can preview your changes, save your preferences and restore the default settings whenever you want.

Display and Text

Adjust how text appears on your screen.

Font Family

Choose a font that is comfortable to read.

Text Size

Select the text size that works best for you.

Color and Display

Improve visibility and reduce distractions.

High Contrast

Use stronger borders and darker text for improved visibility.

Simplified Display

Reduce decorative visual effects and card shadows.

Reduce Motion

Reduce animations and transition effects throughout the Teacher Workspace.

Language Preference

Select your preferred language.

Preferred Language

Your selected language preference will be saved to your settings.

Automatic translation of every screen requires the approved language resources. Selecting a language here saves your preference but does not yet translate all interface content.

Text-to-Speech

Configure your reading and speech preferences.

Enable Text-to-Speech

Allow the reading preview to speak using your browser or device.

Speech Voice

Available voices depend on your browser and device.

What each setting helps you do.

**Readable Text** Adjust the font and text size.

**Language Preference** Save your preferred language.
**Reading Assistance** Use supported browser/device speech synthesis.

Personal Settings

Your preferences are stored separately from other users' settings.

  • Settings apply to your own browser session.
  • Browser storage is used in this frontend demo.
  • Account-wide synchronization will require Django and PostgreSQL integration.

Frontend demonstration only. Preferences are saved locally in this browser. Official account-wide persistence will be handled by the Backend.
