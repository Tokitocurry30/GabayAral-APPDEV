Notification and Read Status Rules
  • One shared event may generate separate notification records for multiple recipients.
  • Each recipient has an independent read status.
  • Marking your notification as read does not change another recipient's notification.
  • Notification history is preserved.
  • Notification source references follow the approved source relationships.
