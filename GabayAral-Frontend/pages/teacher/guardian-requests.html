Teacher Verification Guidelines
  • Verify the guardian against official school enrollment and guardian records.
  • Each guardian must be independently verified for the relevant Student and Section.
  • Refer requests that cannot be independently verified to the authorized School Administrator or registrar.
  • Student approval is not required for guardian connection.
  • Only the authorized School Administrator or registrar may revoke an approved guardian connection.

Frontend demonstration only. Request records and verification actions are not connected to official school records or PostgreSQL.
