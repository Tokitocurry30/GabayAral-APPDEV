Parent Acknowledgment Rules
  • Each acknowledgment belongs to one Guardian account/link and one exact announcement version.
  • Each Guardian acknowledges independently.
  • Material announcement changes require fresh acknowledgment.
  • Minor spelling or formatting corrections do not reset the acknowledgment requirement.
  • Announcement reads and Parent acknowledgments are separate records.

Frontend demonstration only. Acknowledgment records are not connected to official Guardian accounts or PostgreSQL.
