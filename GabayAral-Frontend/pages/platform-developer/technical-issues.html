SECTION C

Investigation Procedure

01. Create Technical Issue Record

Document the issue, affected component and available permitted evidence.

02. Review and Reproduce

Examine the reported behavior and record reproduction steps.

03. Investigate Technical Cause

Identify the likely cause and prepare a proposed correction.

04. Proceed to Review and Testing

Submit changes for independent review and quality assurance.

SECTION D

Investigation Guidelines

Permitted Evidence

Attach only technical information that is authorized for the investigation.

Required

Investigation History

Preserve findings, technical changes, responsible actors and timestamps.

Required

Do not attach passwords, access tokens, Student grades, submissions or private Guardian records to technical issues.
