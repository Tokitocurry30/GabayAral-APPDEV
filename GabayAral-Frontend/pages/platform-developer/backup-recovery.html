SECTION F

Recovery Lifecycle

01. Identify Recovery Concern

Document the backup failure or recovery requirement.

02. Investigate Technical Cause

Review permitted evidence and determine recovery requirements.

03. Obtain Authorization

Verify the applicable recovery authorization and approved procedure.

04. Execute Approved Recovery

Perform the authorized technical recovery procedure.

05. Validate Restored Resources

Verify data integrity and service functionality.

06. Submit Recovery Report

Notify the Platform Administrator and preserve the recovery history.

Recovery execution requires applicable authorization, an approved procedure and documented validation. No live backup provider is assumed.
