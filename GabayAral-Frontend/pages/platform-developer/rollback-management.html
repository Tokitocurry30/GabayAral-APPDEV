SECTION F

Rollback Lifecycle

Standard sequence for investigating and recovering from a failed deployment.

01. Detect Deployment Failure

Stop further release actions and document the deployment failure.

02. Investigate Technical Cause

Review permitted technical evidence and determine whether rollback is required.

03. Verify Authorization

Check the approved or applicable pre-authorized rollback procedure.

04. Execute Authorized Rollback

Follow the approved technical recovery procedure.

05. Validate Previous Stable Version

Verify service recovery and applicable data integrity.

06. Record and Report Results

Notify the Platform Administrator and preserve failure and recovery history.

SECTION G

Rollback Status Definitions

INVESTIGATION Failure is being examined and assessed.
AWAITING AUTHORIZATION Required approval has not yet been confirmed.
EXECUTED Authorized rollback procedure has been performed.
VALIDATED Recovery checks have been completed successfully.

This page does not execute deployment commands, modify production environments or perform rollback. Actual recovery requires the applicable authorization, approved procedure and technical validation.
