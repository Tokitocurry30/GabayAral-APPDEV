Incident Response Workflow

Follow the established administrative and technical coordination process.

01. Security Alert Received

Review the alert and available permitted evidence.

Initial incident assessment
02. Assess Incident Impact

Determine operational impact and whether technical investigation is required.

Platform Administrator responsibility
03. Developer Investigation

Refer technical investigation to an authorized Platform Developer.

Technical investigation and findings
04. Corrective Action

Authorize appropriate remediation and allow the Developer to implement the approved technical correction.

Separation of duties applies
05. Validation and Resolution

Review technical validation and confirm whether the incident has been resolved.

Administrative review
06. Record and Close

Preserve incident evidence, corrective action history and audit information.

Historical record preservation

SECTION F

Incident Review Guidelines

Important controls during incident assessment and response.

Evidence Preservation

Preserve available permitted evidence, relevant event references, technical findings and response history.

Required

Audit History

Record the responsible actor, action, reason, authorization, result and incident status changes.

Required

Separation of Duties

The Developer must not independently authorize their own elevated access or privileged production changes.

Mandatory

Data Protection

Technical incident access must not become unrestricted access to Student grades, submissions or private Guardian documents.

Mandatory
SECTION G

Incident Status Definitions

OPEN Received and awaiting initial assessment
INVESTIGATING Under administrative or technical investigation
ESCALATED Referred to the appropriate higher authority
RESOLVED Corrective action verified and incident resolved
