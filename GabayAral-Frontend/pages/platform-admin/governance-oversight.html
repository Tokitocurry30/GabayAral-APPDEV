SECTION E

Case Closure and Reopening

Review the requirements for case closure and reopening.

Case Closure

Closure requires verification that all requirements and corrective actions are satisfied.

Case Reopening

Reopening requires valid grounds, supporting evidence and independent authorization.

A Senior Platform Administrator cannot approve their own reopening request. Independent authorization is required. If no independent Senior Platform Administrator is available, use the existing higher authorized governance authority.

Overdue directions do not automatically suspend or remove Platform Administrator permissions.
