ABOUT GABAYARAL

One Platform for a Connected School Community

GabayAral is designed to support communication and learning activity management across multiple schools while keeping school information and academic records properly organized.

Multi-School Management

Separate school workspaces with organized memberships, academic structures and access permissions.

Connected Community

Support communication among School Administrators, Teachers, Students and Parents/Guardians.

Learning Management

Organize activities, submissions, quizzes, feedback and academic progress monitoring.

PLATFORM FEATURES

Tools That Support Teaching and Learning

Announcements

GABAYARAL USERS

Designed for Six User Roles

Each role has its own workspace, responsibilities and authorized features.

School Administrator

Manages school structure, memberships, offerings, assignments and authorized school operations.

Teacher

Manages assigned subjects, announcements, activities, quizzes, submissions, grades and Guardian verification.

Student

Joins assigned offerings, accesses learning activities, submits work, takes quizzes and views released grades.

Parent / Guardian

Connects with eligible children and monitors authorized announcements, acknowledgments and academic progress.

Platform Administrator

Oversees platform governance, workspace authorization, incidents and operational monitoring.

Platform Developer

Performs authorized technical maintenance, testing, releases and recovery operations.
